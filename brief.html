Learnen.edu - Creative Brief

Presented by Sarah Houssein & Mahamed Abdel

Brand Overview

Industry: Education Technology - Math Tutoring Services

Mission Statement: To make math engaging and accessible for students of all ages.

Brand History: Founded in 2024, Learnen.edu has evolved from a small tutoring business into a comprehensive platform offering innovative tools for math learning.

Core Values: Innovation, Accessibility, Empowerment, Passion for Education

Target Audience

Age Range: Students ages 8-18, parents, and educators

Interests: Improving math skills, engaging with fun learning tools

Needs: Personalized tutoring, easy-to-use math resources

Brand Identity

Essence: Making math fun and accessible for everyone.

Key Attributes: Fun, engaging, accessible to all levels, supportive and empowering.

Emotional Response: Confidence, curiosity, and enthusiasm for learning math.

Product/Service Information

Main Offerings: Online math tutoring, interactive math games, personalized learning plans, educational videos and resources.

Unique Selling Proposition: Combines personalized learning with fun, interactive tools, standing out from traditional tutoring.

Key Benefits: Builds confidence, makes math engaging, accessible on all devices.
